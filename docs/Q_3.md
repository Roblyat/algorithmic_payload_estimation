### 1. **Extended Deep Lagrangian Network for Robotic Arm Dynamics considering Motor Couplings

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Standard DeLaN assumes ideal actuation, ignoring motor friction/coupling. This reduces model accuracy in real robots.

* **SoA**:

  * Physics-based models (Lagrangian, Newton–Euler): interpretable but parameter-sensitive.
  * Pure DL models: flexible but data-hungry & uninterpretable.
  * Physics-inspired NN hybrids (DeLaN) are promising but incomplete (ignore motor torque effects).

* **Math Background**:

  * Lagrangian mechanics (H, C, g terms).
  * NN parameterizations of energy functions.
  * Motor friction & coupling dynamics.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **Extended DeLaN**: integrates motor signals (current/voltage) + robot motion data.
  * Learns hybrid model: physics constraints + motor torque modeling.

* **Results**:

  * Tested on **UR10e 6-DoF arm**.
  * Outperformed both physics-only and data-only models in simulation & experiments.
  * Captures nonlinearities like friction torque and motor couplings.

* **Contribution**:

  * Extends DeLaN framework with motor coupling effects.
  * Enables higher accuracy dynamics modeling for real robots.

* **Outlook**:

  * Apply to payload variation & contact tasks.
  * Explore transfer learning across robots.

* **Focus**:
  **Internal dynamics modeling (with motor effects)**, useful for both control and parameter identification.

### 2. **Parameter Identification of a Robot Arm Manipulator Based on a Convolutional Neural Network** (De León et al., *IEEE Access*, 2022)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Identifying inertial parameters (mass, CoM, inertia tensor) is hard due to **noise, unmodeled dynamics, and trajectory design requirements**. CNNs offer potential to map signals directly to parameters.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: LS, RLS, EKF for parameter ID.
  * Learning: Neural networks, mostly MLPs and RNNs, rarely CNNs.

* **Math Background**:

  * Robot dynamics identification problem: Y(q, q̇, q̈)β = τ.
  * CNNs as feature extractors from temporal/spatial signals.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **CNN-based architecture** for dynamic parameter identification.
  * Input: joint trajectories + torque signals.
  * Output: dynamic parameter vector (β).
  * Trained with supervised dataset of simulated and real data.

* **Results**:

  * CNN achieved robust identification even under noise.
  * Outperformed LS and some NN baselines.
  * Showed resilience against sensor errors.

* **Contribution**:

  * First use of CNNs for robot arm parameter ID.
  * Demonstrated CNNs can replace classical regression for payload dynamics.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to online/real-time identification.
  * Integrate CNN feature extraction with hybrid physics-informed approaches.

* **Focus**:
  **Dynamic parameter identification (β vector)** with CNN.

### 3. **A Learning-Based Approach for Estimating Inertial Properties of Unknown Objects from Encoder Discrepancies** (IEEE RAL 2023)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  F/T sensors are costly & unsuitable for small robots. Need sensorless estimation of mass & CoM of unknown payloads.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: regressors with F/T sensors.
  * Some encoder-based ID, but rarely accurate without torque sensors.

* **Math Background**:

  * Relation between joint torques & payload inertia.
  * Weighted least squares.
  * Neural nets for joint torque estimation.
  * Attention mechanisms for dynamic weighting.

* **Methods**:

  * Robot carries unknown object through multiple configurations.
  * Neural net estimates torques from **encoder discrepancies** (desired vs actual joint angles).
  * Attention model assigns dynamic weights to torque errors.
  * Solve for payload **mass + CoM** via weighted least squares.

* **Results**:

  * Demonstrated on **4-DoF arm**.
  * Achieved accurate mass & CoM estimation without F/T sensors.
  * Attention mechanism improved robustness by emphasizing informative joints.

* **Contribution**:

  * Novel **encoder-only payload estimation** framework.
  * Introduces attention-based weighting for inertial property inference.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to multi-DoF arms, online estimation.
  * Apply to collaborative robots in unstructured environments.

* **Focus**:
  **Payload identification (mass + CoM)** without F/T sensors.

### 4. **End-Effector Force and Joint Torque Estimation of a 7-DoF Robotic Manipulator Using Deep Learning** (Kružić et al., *Electronics*, 2021)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Analytical inverse dynamics models are inaccurate under **noise and unmodeled dynamics**. There is a need for **robust force/torque estimation** methods without relying on costly sensors.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: Euler–Lagrange models, observers (momentum, disturbance).
  * Learning: Neural networks (MLP, CNN, LSTM), DeLaN (physics-informed).

* **Math Background**:

  * Robot inverse dynamics: τ = H(q)q̈ + C(q, q̇) + g(q) + τf(q̇).
  * Neural sequence learning (esp. LSTM).

* **Methods**:

  * Compared MLP, CNN, and LSTM architectures.
  * Inputs: joint positions, velocities, accelerations.
  * Outputs: joint torques & end-effector forces.
  * Evaluated vs. analytical ID models and DeLaN.

* **Results**:

  * **LSTM performed best**, capturing temporal dependencies.
  * Robust in real-world data (Franka Emika Panda, \~480 min).
  * Outperformed feedforward NN and analytic models.

* **Contribution**:

  * First systematic comparison of multiple DL architectures for force/torque estimation.
  * Highlighted **LSTMs as state of the art** for sequential robot dynamics learning.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to online adaptive estimation.
  * Combine with uncertainty quantification.

* **Focus**:
  **Force/torque estimation** with **deep sequence models**.

### 5. **Neural-Learning-Based Force Sensorless Admittance Control for Robots With Input Deadzone** (T-IE 2021)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Accurate force estimation is challenging without physical F/T sensors. Input deadzone nonlinearities of actuators degrade tracking and stability in interactive control.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: disturbance observers, dynamic state observers, momentum observers for external torque estimation.
  * NN-enhanced observers: multilayer feedforward or RVFLNNs, but require accurate system dynamics and suffer high computational cost.

* **Math Background**:

  * Robot dynamics: $M(q)\ddot{q} + C(q,\dot{q})\dot{q} + G(q) = \tau + J^T f_{ext}$.
  * Deadzone nonlinear function $D(\tau)$ introduces control errors.
  * Admittance control maps estimated external torque → compliant trajectory.
  * Lyapunov-based stability proofs ensure boundedness of errors.

* **Methods**:

  * Introduces **flatted RBF neural network (RBFNN)** with incremental learning to estimate external torque (sensorless).
  * Dynamic RBFNN controller compensates system uncertainties + actuator deadzone.
  * Admittance control framework constrains robot motion using a saturation function.
  * Experiments on Baxter robot validate external torque estimation + compliant motion control.

* **Results**:

  * Achieved accurate external torque estimation without F/T sensors.
  * RBFNN (1000 nodes) tracked nonlinear function dynamics well with low training time (\~5s).
  * Effective trajectory adaptation under external disturbance (12–30s test window).
  * Stable, compliant interaction demonstrated with admittance control + NN observer.

* **Contribution**:

  * First integration of **incremental-learning RBFNN** with **admittance control** for **force sensorless compliant robotics**.
  * Compensates actuator deadzone nonlinearities while reducing computational overhead.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend framework to multi-contact environments.
  * Explore lightweight NN structures for real-time high-DoF robots.

* **Focus**:
  **Sensorless external force estimation + compliant admittance control using NN**.

### 6 **An Adaptive Sparse General Regression Neural Network-Based Force Observer for Teleoperation System** (EAAI 2023)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Teleoperation robots in surgery, nuclear cleanup, or hazardous environments often cannot use **force/torque sensors** due to size, cost, or safety. Accurate force estimation without sensors is critical, but traditional model-based methods require accurate robot dynamics, which are unavailable.

* **SoA**:

  * **Classical**: inverse dynamics + observers (Kalman, DOB) → depend on accurate models.
  * **Learning-based**: GPR, MINN (model-independent NN), RF ensembles.
  * Problems: high-dimensional input features, redundant support vectors, poor generalization.

* **Math Background**:

  * **General Regression Neural Network (GRNN)** – kernel regression with bandwidth parameter ℎ.
  * Sparse regularization + feature selection to reduce input dimensionality.
  * Support vector pruning (fixed pruning rate, Euclidean distance threshold).
  * **Improved Ant Lion Optimization (IALO)** – optimizes GRNN bandwidth.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **Adaptive Sparse GRNN (ASGRNN)** force observer.
  * Innovations:

    * Wrapper + sparse regularization feature selection.
    * Two support vector pruning rules (fixed-rate, distance-threshold).
    * Improved ALO (better random walk, weighted updates, opposition-based learning).
  * Inputs: joint current, position, velocity, acceleration.
  * Training: offline with F/T sensor (UR5e slave robot, Omega\_7 master). Sensor removed in actual deployment.

* **Results**:

  * Tested in **soft (foam)** and **stiff (plastic)** environments.
  * Compared vs. **GPR, MINN, Random Forest**.
  * ASGRNN reduced **MSE by up to 81.6%** and MAE by \~56% in stiff environments.
  * Robust to noise, highest transparency in teleoperation.
  * Achieved real-time performance with optimized pruning.

* **Contribution**:

  * First **ASGRNN-based force observer** for teleoperation, sensorless and model-free.
  * Combines feature sparsification + metaheuristic optimization for efficient, accurate estimation.
  * Applicable to harsh environments where F/T sensors are impractical.

* **Outlook**:

  * Translate estimated forces into master-side joint space for **haptic feedback**.
  * Explore adaptive online retraining to compensate for wear/aging.
  * Compare with classical observers (e.g., adaptive KF).

* **Focus**:
  **Force/torque estimation for teleoperation without sensors**, using sparse neural regression + metaheuristic optimization.
