### 1. **Uncertainty in Bayesian Reinforcement Learning for Robot Manipulation Tasks with Sparse Rewards** (IEEE ROBIO 2023)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Conventional deep RL struggles with sparse-reward manipulation tasks. Agents explore poorly, converge slowly, and are unstable under uncertainty.

* **SoA**:

  * RL widely applied in grasping, tool use, HRI.
  * Bayesian Neural Networks (BNNs) offer uncertainty estimates but scale poorly.
  * Dropout-based Bayesian approximations introduced to make DRL uncertainty-aware.

* **Math Background**:

  * MDPs, value functions, policy gradients.
  * Bayesian inference (priors, posteriors over NN weights).
  * KL divergence for reward uncertainty.
  * Monte Carlo sampling (MCMC).

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **Bayesian Deep RL (BDRL)** framework.
  * Quantifies **3 types of uncertainty**:

    1. **Model uncertainty** (NN weights).
    2. **Aleatoric uncertainty** (data noise).
    3. **Reward function uncertainty** (mis-specified sparse rewards).
  * Combines DRL + Bayesian network for action selection & uncertainty-aware training.

* **Results**:

  * Tested on **4 robot manipulation tasks** (Reach, Push, Pick-and-Place, Slide).
  * Improved convergence, stability, and exploration compared to vanilla DRL.
  * Bayesian networks enabled **conservative actions under uncertainty**, reducing failure.

* **Contribution**:

  * Introduces **uncertainty-aware RL for manipulation** under sparse rewards.
  * Provides generalizable method to accelerate training and stabilize policies.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to real-world manipulation & HRI.
  * Improve scalability of Bayesian approximations for larger networks.

* **Focus**:
  **Learning-based policy optimization**, not direct parameter ID or force estimation.

### 2. **Extended Deep Lagrangian Network (DeLaN) for Robotic Arm Dynamics Considering Motor Couplings** (IEEE, 2025)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Standard DeLaN assumes ideal actuation, ignoring motor friction/coupling. This reduces model accuracy in real robots.

* **SoA**:

  * Physics-based models (Lagrangian, Newton–Euler): interpretable but parameter-sensitive.
  * Pure DL models: flexible but data-hungry & uninterpretable.
  * Physics-inspired NN hybrids (DeLaN) are promising but incomplete (ignore motor torque effects).

* **Math Background**:

  * Lagrangian mechanics (H, C, g terms).
  * NN parameterizations of energy functions.
  * Motor friction & coupling dynamics.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **Extended DeLaN**: integrates motor signals (current/voltage) + robot motion data.
  * Learns hybrid model: physics constraints + motor torque modeling.

* **Results**:

  * Tested on **UR10e 6-DoF arm**.
  * Outperformed both physics-only and data-only models in simulation & experiments.
  * Captures nonlinearities like friction torque and motor couplings.

* **Contribution**:

  * Extends DeLaN framework with motor coupling effects.
  * Enables higher accuracy dynamics modeling for real robots.

* **Outlook**:

  * Apply to payload variation & contact tasks.
  * Explore transfer learning across robots.

* **Focus**:
  **Internal dynamics modeling (with motor effects)**, useful for both control and parameter identification.

### 3. **Parameter Identification of a Robot Arm Manipulator Based on a CNN** (IEEE Access 2022)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Traditional parameter identification (least-squares, regressor-based) requires clean, full signals. Noise and limited data degrade performance.

* **SoA**:

  * Subspace ID, least squares, EKF.
  * Learning-based ID (NNs) emerging but underexplored for manipulator parameters.

* **Math Background**:

  * Robot dynamics (τ = f(q, q̇, q̈, β)).
  * CNNs for feature extraction from temporal signals (subsampled by DCT-II).

* **Methods**:

  * Converts robot state + torque signals into **images** via DCT-based subsampling.
  * CNN extracts features → estimates parameters β (inertial set).
  * Uses backpropagation with ReLU activations, sigmoid output.

* **Results**:

  * Trained/tested on **2-DoF robot arm**.
  * CNN accurately identified dynamic parameters.
  * Outperformed conventional regression under noisy signals.

* **Contribution**:

  * Introduces **vision-inspired CNN approach** for robot parameter ID.
  * Handles noise and reduces dimensionality with signal-to-image transformation.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to higher-DoF robots.
  * Embed CNN-based ID for online control adaptation.

* **Focus**:
  **Payload/parameter identification** via deep learning.

### 4. **A Learning-Based Approach for Estimating Inertial Properties of Unknown Objects from Encoder Discrepancies** (IEEE RAL 2023)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  F/T sensors are costly & unsuitable for small robots. Need sensorless estimation of mass & CoM of unknown payloads.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: regressors with F/T sensors.
  * Some encoder-based ID, but rarely accurate without torque sensors.

* **Math Background**:

  * Relation between joint torques & payload inertia.
  * Weighted least squares.
  * Neural nets for joint torque estimation.
  * Attention mechanisms for dynamic weighting.

* **Methods**:

  * Robot carries unknown object through multiple configurations.
  * Neural net estimates torques from **encoder discrepancies** (desired vs actual joint angles).
  * Attention model assigns dynamic weights to torque errors.
  * Solve for payload **mass + CoM** via weighted least squares.

* **Results**:

  * Demonstrated on **4-DoF arm**.
  * Achieved accurate mass & CoM estimation without F/T sensors.
  * Attention mechanism improved robustness by emphasizing informative joints.

* **Contribution**:

  * Novel **encoder-only payload estimation** framework.
  * Introduces attention-based weighting for inertial property inference.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to multi-DoF arms, online estimation.
  * Apply to collaborative robots in unstructured environments.

* **Focus**:
  **Payload identification (mass + CoM)** without F/T sensors.

### 5. **End-Effector Force and Joint Torque Estimation of a 7-DoF Robotic Manipulator Using Deep Learning** (Electronics, 2021)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Need accurate force/torque estimation for control & safety. Analytical inverse dynamics models degrade under noise/unmodeled dynamics.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: Euler–Lagrange inverse dynamics, observers.
  * Learning-based: neural nets (MLP, CNN, LSTM).

* **Math Background**:

  * Robot inverse dynamics: τ = H(q)q̈ + C(q, q̇) + g(q) + τf(q̇).
  * Neural architectures for sequence learning (LSTM).

* **Methods**:

  * Trained multiple architectures (MLP, CNN, LSTM).
  * Inputs: joint positions, velocities, accelerations; outputs: torques/forces.
  * Compared to analytical models + DeLaN.

* **Results**:

  * LSTM networks performed best for **end-effector force estimation**.
  * Robust under real-world experiments (Franka 7-DoF, \~480 min data).
  * Showed advantage of sequential architectures over feedforward nets.

* **Contribution**:

  * Systematic study of DL architectures for force/torque estimation.
  * Showed **LSTMs best capture temporal dependencies** in robot dynamics.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to online adaptive estimation.
  * Combine with probabilistic models for uncertainty quantification.

* **Focus**:
  **Force/torque estimation** using deep sequence models.
