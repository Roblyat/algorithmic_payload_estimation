### 1. **Augmenting Tactile Simulators with Real-like and Zero-Shot Capabilities (SightGAN)** (ICRA, 2024)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Tactile simulators have a *reality gap* — synthetic tactile images differ significantly from real ones. Models trained purely in sim often fail zero-shot transfer to real sensors.

* **SoA**:

  * Domain randomization, FEM-based deformation models.
  * GAN-based sim-to-real transfer (CycleGAN, RetinaGAN variants).

* **Math Background**:

  * GAN adversarial loss: $L_{GAN}$.
  * CycleGAN cycle consistency: $L_{cycle}$.
  * SightGAN adds:

    * **Spatial Contact Consistency loss**: ensures accurate contact localization.
    * **Pixel-wise Contact Region Consistency loss**: ensures background + contact trace fidelity.
  * Loss = CycleGAN + auxiliary terms.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **SightGAN** (CycleGAN + contact-specific residual losses).
  * Input: difference images (contact − reference) from **AllSight tactile sensor**.
  * Bidirectional mapping (sim↔real).
  * Contact position estimation via ResNet-18 on generated images.
  * Evaluated transfer to unseen sensors + indenters.

* **Results**:

  * Position RMSE reduced from \~13.3 mm (CycleGAN) → 3.49 mm (SightGAN).
  * 47% improvement in FID over CycleGAN.
  * **Zero-shot inference** possible for unseen sensors.
  * Retained embedded **force information** in tactile images.

* **Contribution**:

  * Introduced **residual contact-aware losses** into CycleGAN, improving sim-to-real tactile transfer.
  * First to show robust **zero-shot tactile inference** on **3D round sensors**.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to multi-modal tactile sensors (RGB lighting, different geometries).
  * Combine with RL for policy learning directly on real-like simulated tactile data.

* **Focus**:
  **GAN-based hybrid residual model** for **tactile sim-to-real transfer** and **contact/force inference**.

### 2. **Programmatic Imitation Learning from Unlabeled and Noisy Demonstrations (PLUNDER)** (RA-L, 2024)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Imitation Learning (IL) often requires *labeled*, *noise-free* demos and produces black-box neural policies. These are hard to interpret and adapt.

* **SoA**:

  * Behavior Cloning (NN-based).
  * Inverse RL.
  * Programmatic Imitation Learning (PIL): symbolic program synthesis (LDIPS, PROLEX).
  * GAN-based IfO (GAIfO) for unlabeled demos — but opaque policies.

* **Math Background**:

  * Formulated as **latent variable MAP estimation**:
    $\pi^* = \arg \max_{\pi} \sum_{a_{1:t}} P(z|a,s)P(a|s,\pi)P(\pi)$.
  * Uses **Expectation-Maximization (EM)**:

    * E-step: infer action labels via particle filter.
    * M-step: synthesize probabilistic ASP (Action Selection Policy).
  * Prior: penalizes large ASTs to prevent overfitting.

* **Methods**:

  * **PLUNDER algorithm**: probabilistic PIL with EM loop.
  * Synthesizes interpretable **probabilistic programs** in DSL form.
  * Handles **noisy, unlabeled** human or sim demonstrations.
  * Compared against LDIPS, BC/BC+, GAIfO, Behavior Transformers.

* **Results**:

  * 95% alignment with demos (19% better than next best).
  * 90% task success rate (17% higher).
  * Converges in <10 EM iterations.
  * Robust under noise (outperforms GAIfO & BC).
  * Generated policies interpretable (conditions expressed in logic + probabilities).

* **Contribution**:

  * First **probabilistic PIL** for noisy, unlabeled demos.
  * Bridges gap between **symbolic program synthesis** and **probabilistic modeling**.

* **Outlook**:

  * Apply to **real-world robot data**.
  * Integrate **LLMs** or **neural-guided synthesis** for scalability.
  * Jointly optimize observation models.

* **Focus**:
  **Residual hybridization** of **program synthesis + probabilistic models** → robust, interpretable **robot learning from demos**.
