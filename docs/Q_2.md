### 1. **Decoupling Observer for Contact Force Estimation of Robot Manipulators Based on Enhanced Gaussian Process Model** (CCIS 2022)

* **Problem/Issue**: Force estimation without force sensors is highly dependent on accurate robot dynamics models, which are often imperfect in practice. Existing observers (GMO, DOB, NDOB) struggle with residual dynamics and noise.
* **SoA**: Classical sensorless force observers (GMO, NDOB, DOB). Neural networks and GP have been used for residual dynamics but often ignore uncertainty.
* **Math Background**: Euler–Lagrange dynamics, generalized momentum, Gaussian process regression (SE kernel, MAP estimation), Bayesian filtering.
* **Methods**:

  * Hybrid **Enhanced GP (EGP)** model: nominal dynamics + GPR-learned residual dynamics.
  * Developed **GPDKF (Gaussian Process Disturbance Kalman Filter)**: decouples contact force from residual dynamics, uses GPR mean & covariance in filtering.
* **Results**:

  * Robust, accurate contact force estimation in experiments with a 3-DoF manipulator.
  * Outperforms traditional DKF and NN-based EDKF, especially under noise and model errors.
* **Contribution**: Introduces uncertainty-aware semi-parametric observer (EGP+GPR+Kalman filtering) for real-time contact force estimation.
* **Outlook**: Extend to higher-DoF robots and unstructured environments; explore sparse GPs for efficiency.
* **Focus**: **Contact force estimation (not payload ID).**

### 2. **Contact Force Estimation of Robot Manipulators With Imperfect Dynamic Model: On Gaussian Process Adaptive Disturbance Kalman Filter** (IEEE T-ASE 2024)

* **Problem/Issue**: Contact force estimation under **multi-source uncertainties** (modeling errors, noise, unknown contact dynamics). Traditional inverse dynamics or DKF degrade under imperfect models.
* **SoA**:

  * Classical: physics-based (Euler–Lagrange + ID), DKF, GMO, NDO, DOB.
  * Hybrid: NNs for residual dynamics, but poor uncertainty quantification.
* **Math Background**:

  * Semi-parametric dynamic modeling (nominal + residual).
  * **GPR** for residual dynamics (mean + covariance).
  * **Variational Bayes (VB)** to adapt noise covariance in Kalman filter.
* **Methods**:

  * Proposed **GPADKF (Gaussian Process Adaptive Disturbance Kalman Filter)**.
  * Uses GPR for residual dynamics compensation and uncertainty quantification.
  * VB updates force dynamics noise statistics online.
* **Results**:

  * Significantly improved accuracy and reliability vs. EDKF and DKF.
  * Effective in highly uncertain environments (non-industrial robots, actuator degradation).
* **Contribution**: Unified framework (CDF → GPADKF) combining GPR and VB for robust force estimation.
* **Outlook**: Apply to complex contact-rich tasks (assembly, surgical robotics). Improve scalability with sparse GP methods.
* **Focus**: **Contact force estimation (not payload ID).**

### 3. **Human-Robot Contact Detection in Assembly Tasks** (2022)

* **Problem/Issue**:
  In collaborative assembly, safe physical interaction requires reliable **contact detection** between human and robot.
  Traditional force/torque sensors or purely model-based observers are either costly or unreliable under uncertainty.

* **SoA**:

  * Contact detection methods:

    * Thresholding on force/torque sensors.
    * Model-based disturbance observers (DOB, GMO, momentum observer).
    * Learning-based classifiers.
  * Limitations: sensitivity to noise, poor generalization to complex assembly environments.

* **Math Background**:

  * Probabilistic modeling of contact events.
  * Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) for mapping sensory signals to contact/non-contact.
  * Bayesian decision theory for classification.

* **Methods**:

  * Proposes a **GP-based probabilistic contact detection framework**.
  * Uses robot proprioceptive data + interaction signals.
  * GPR models are trained to classify contact vs. free motion.
  * Outputs both prediction and confidence (uncertainty).

* **Results**:

  * Demonstrated in **assembly task experiments** (human inserting or guiding parts with robot).
  * Outperforms deterministic classifiers in accuracy and robustness.
  * Probabilistic confidence helps prevent false positives (spurious contacts).

* **Contribution**:

  * Introduces **uncertainty-aware GP model** for safe human-robot interaction in assembly.
  * Moves beyond thresholding → provides a probabilistic framework.

* **Outlook**:

  * Extend to multi-modal sensing (vision + proprioception).
  * Real-time online adaptation to varying contact conditions.

* **Focus**:
  **Contact detection (binary classification: contact vs. no contact)**.
  This is not parameter identification but is closely related to **payload/contact safety in collaborative tasks**.

## 🔎 Conclusion of Q2 Research (Gaussian Process / Hybrid GP)

### 1. **Scope Covered**

* Q₂ research focuses almost exclusively on **contact force estimation** and **contact detection**.
* The central idea: use **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)** to model residual robot dynamics and compensate for uncertainties.
* Some works integrate GPR with **Kalman filtering** or **variational Bayesian inference** for robust estimation.

---

### 2. **What They Estimate**

* **Internal robot parameters / contact forces**:

  * *Decoupling Observer + Enhanced GP (EGP + GPDKF)* → estimates **contact forces** by separating residual dynamics from physical interaction.
  * *GP Adaptive Disturbance Kalman Filter (GPADKF)* → estimates **contact forces** under multi-source uncertainty.
  * *Learning to Estimate Forces for Safe Tool Usage* (not yet summarized in detail) → maps sensory data to **interaction forces** for safety in tool use.
  * *Human-Robot Contact Detection in Assembly Tasks* → detects **contact events (binary classification)** using probabilistic GP models.

* **Payload / Object parameters**:

  * None of the surveyed Q₂ papers explicitly estimate payload inertial parameters (mass, CoM, inertia).
  * All contributions remain within **force/contact estimation** for safe interaction, not payload ID.

---

### 3. **Mathematical Backbone**

* **Gaussian Process Regression (GPR)**:

  * Non-parametric Bayesian regression to model residual dynamics.
  * Uses mean + covariance for uncertainty-aware predictions.

* **Observers & Filtering**:

  * GPR combined with **Kalman Filtering** (GPDKF, GPADKF).
  * Variational Bayes (VB) to adapt noise covariance online.

* **Classification**:

  * GP models extended to **probabilistic contact detection** (binary classification with uncertainty).

---

### 4. **State of the Art Trend**

* Strong trend toward **uncertainty-aware estimation**: GPR provides not only a mean estimate but also confidence intervals.
* Move from **deterministic observers** → **probabilistic hybrid models** (GP + KF, GP + VB).
* Expansion from continuous force estimation → into **contact event detection** for human-robot collaboration.

---

### 5. **Overall Coverage**

* **Robot Internal Estimation**:

  * Yes: Contact force estimation (via GP hybrid observers).
  * Extends to binary contact detection (probabilistic classification).

* **Payload Estimation**:

  * No: None of the surveyed works estimate payload inertial parameters.
  * Contact force estimation is relevant for payload interaction but not direct identification.

---

✅ **Conclusion:**
Q₂ (Gaussian Process / Hybrid GP) research covers **robust, uncertainty-aware contact force estimation and detection**, not payload inertial identification.
The frontier lies in **hybrid GPR frameworks (GP + KF + VB)** that improve robustness against model error and noise.
These works are highly relevant to **safety and interaction tasks** in cobots, but do not yet bridge into **payload parameter estimation**. Future directions may combine GP-based force estimation with **payload ID frameworks** to unify internal and external parameter estimation.
