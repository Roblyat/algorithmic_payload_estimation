### 1. **Tactile-Driven Dexterous In-Hand Writing via Extrinsic Contact Sensing** (RA-L 2025)

* **Problem/Issue**: In-hand manipulation requires precise contact force sensing. Standard tactile sensors have limited coverage → difficult to generalize to dexterous tasks like writing.

* **SoA**: Reinforcement learning with tactile inputs; extrinsic sensors improve generalization.

* **Math Background**: Policy learning via RL, tactile sensing as state features, trajectory optimization.

* **Methods**:

  * Extrinsic tactile sensing (contact sensors outside the hand).
  * RL framework learns dexterous writing policy.
  * Adaptation to variability in handwriting and surfaces.

* **Results**: Achieved robust in-hand writing on surfaces, adapting to contact dynamics.

* **Contribution**: Demonstrates extrinsic tactile sensing + RL for **dexterous in-hand tasks**.

* **Outlook**: Generalize to broader in-hand manipulation (tool use, assembly).

* **Focus**: **Contact/task adaptation**, not payload ID.

### 2. **Optimization of Adaptive Algorithm for Precise Motion Control of Multi-DoF Robotic Arms** (ICETAC 2025)

* **Problem/Issue**: Precise control of multi-DoF arms suffers under uncertainty, noise, and nonlinearities.

* **SoA**: Fuzzy control, heuristic optimization, DRL for real-time adaptation.

* **Math Background**: Adaptive control, fuzzy logic, online optimization, DRL policy learning.

* **Methods**:

  * Combined fuzzy control + deep reinforcement learning.
  * Optimized adaptive algorithm for trajectory tracking.

* **Results**: Improved accuracy + robustness in real-time control.

* **Contribution**: Domain adaptation method for **high-precision trajectory control**.

* **Outlook**: Extend to contact-rich manipulation.

* **Focus**: **Robot control adaptation**, not parameter ID.

### 3. **Sim-to-Real Transfer of Bolting Tasks with Tight Tolerance** (IROS 2020)

* **Problem/Issue**: Bolting tasks need **tight tolerance**; sim-to-real gap prevents policy transfer.

* **SoA**: Domain randomization, adaptation strategies.

* **Math Background**: Dynamics modeling, sim-to-real transfer, RL adaptation.

* **Methods**:

  * Domain randomization in simulation.
  * Transferred RL-trained policy to real-world bolting.

* **Results**: Successful transfer, high precision in real bolting tasks.

* **Contribution**: First to show sim-to-real bolting task success.

* **Outlook**: Extend to other industrial assembly tasks.

* **Focus**: **Domain adaptation for manipulation policies**.

### 4. **Sampling-Based MPC Leveraging Parallelizable Physics Simulations** (RA-L 2025)

* **Problem/Issue**: Online MPC is limited by computational cost.

* **SoA**: Sampling-based MPC, physics simulators.

* **Math Background**: MPC, contact dynamics, optimization.

* **Methods**:

  * Physics simulations parallelized for sampling-based MPC.
  * Contact modeling integrated into optimization.

* **Results**: Improved real-time MPC, robust to contact.

* **Contribution**: Framework uniting **contact dynamics + MPC + physics simulation**.

* **Outlook**: Whole-body manipulation with fast online MPC.

* **Focus**: **Control adaptation with simulation-in-the-loop**.

### 5. **Contact Localization for Robot Arms in Motion without Torque Sensing** (ICRA 2021)

* **Problem/Issue**: Contact localization usually requires torque sensing.

* **SoA**: Contact particle filter, SVM classifiers, ML models with proprioception.

* **Math Background**: Domain randomization, convolutional neural networks, cylindrical projection encoding.

* **Methods**:

  * Neural network trained with domain randomization.
  * Cylindrical projection encoding → convolutional feature extraction.
  * Predicts contact points as distance fields.

* **Results**: 91.5% detection accuracy, 3.0 cm localization error.

* **Contribution**: Enables contact localization without torque sensors.

* **Outlook**: Extend to multi-contact, cluttered environments.

* **Focus**: **Contact localization via proprioception**.

---

### 6. **A Transfer Learning Approach to Cross-Modal Object Recognition** (T-RO 2019)

* **Problem/Issue**: Robots need to recognize objects using different modalities (vision vs. touch).

* **SoA**: Visual-only or tactile-only recognition; multimodal still underexplored.

* **Math Background**: Transfer learning, multimodal representation, cross-domain features.

* **Methods**:

  * Unified visuo-tactile representation (point clouds).
  * Feature equalization (MLS, voxel filters).
  * Transfer learning across domains.

* **Results**: 94.7% recognition accuracy, comparable to monomodal.

* **Contribution**: First visuo-tactile cross-modal recognition with transfer learning.

* **Outlook**: Scale to larger object sets, online adaptation.

* **Focus**: **Cross-modal payload/object recognition**.

## 🔎 Conclusion of Q₆ Research (Domain Adaptation / Latent Context)

### 1. **Scope Covered**

* Covers **domain adaptation, sim-to-real transfer, cross-modal perception, tactile-driven adaptation, and MPC with simulation**.
* All papers focus on **adapting policies or perception across domains or modalities**.

### 2. **What They Estimate**

* **Robot internal/contact adaptation**: torque-free contact localization, adaptive motion control.
* **Payload/object recognition**: cross-modal (vision ↔ tactile).
* **Policy adaptation**: sim-to-real bolting, tactile-driven RL, MPC.

### 3. **Mathematical Backbone**

* Domain randomization, transfer learning, reinforcement learning.
* Neural architectures (CNNs with cylindrical projections).
* MPC + physics-informed simulation.
* Multimodal point cloud alignment (MLS, voxel filtering).

### 4. **State of the Art Trend**

* Moving from **direct parameter estimation** → toward **robustness via adaptation**.
* Focus on **safety and real-world applicability**: sim-to-real, tactile robustness, torque-free sensing.
* Multimodal transfer (vision ↔ touch) is an emerging frontier.

### 5. **Overall Coverage**

* **Robot internal adaptation**: control precision, contact localization.
* **Payload/object recognition**: visuo-tactile transfer.
* **Policy-level adaptation**: sim-to-real, RL adaptation, MPC.

✅ **Conclusion**:
Q₆ research does not focus on estimating explicit inertial/payload parameters, but instead on **ensuring estimators and policies generalize across domains, modalities, and uncertainties**. This is crucial for real-world deployment of the estimation pipelines from Q₁–Q₅.
