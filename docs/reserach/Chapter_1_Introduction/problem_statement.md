## 1.2 Problem Statement
Despite extensive work across Q1–Q6, current methods leave three persistent gaps:
(1) **Online PDPI under realistic noise/friction** without strong PE is unreliable [Q1_5, Q1_12, Q4_3].
(2) **Model-lite robustness**: data-driven variants generalize poorly OOD and need heavy calibration [Q3_2, Q3_7, Q6_1].
(3) **Sensor fusion for mass/CoM/inertia** with commodity signals (q, ẋ, τ, F/T, vision) is brittle at low SNR [Q2_1, Q5_4].

Therefore, there is a need for a method that achieves **online PDPI** with **noise/friction robustness** and **weak-excitation tolerance**, while remaining **lightweight in modeling effort**.
