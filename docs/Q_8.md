## 🔎 Q8 Research (Domain Adaptation / Latent Context)

### 1. **Lee et al. (Annual Review of Control 2024)**

**“Robot Model Identification and Learning: A Modern Perspective.”**

* **Problem/Issue**:
  Growing need for robust robot model identification that bridges physics-based and data-driven approaches.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical: LS, RLS, rigid-body dynamics ID.
  * Modern: deep learning, PINNs, hybrid models.

* **Math Background**:

  * System identification theory, parameter identifiability.
  * Statistical parameter estimation vs. physics-informed learning.

* **Methods**:

  * Survey of state-of-the-art methods (geometry-informed, physics-informed ML, sim-to-real).

* **Results**:

  * Provides taxonomy of approaches and their tradeoffs.

* **Contribution**:

  * Unified review bridging **system ID** in robotics and ML.
  * Emphasizes hybrid and geometry-informed models.

### 2. **Leboutet et al. (Applied Sciences 2021)**

**“Inertial Parameter Identification in Robotics: A Survey.”**

* **Problem/Issue**:
  No unified overview of inertial parameter ID techniques.

* **SoA**:

  * Classical LS, RLS, excitation trajectory design.
  * Modern: convex optimization, probabilistic ID.

* **Math Background**:

  * Rigid-body dynamics, identifiability, excitation trajectory observability.

* **Methods**:

  * Systematic survey of parameter ID methods.

* **Results**:

  * Benchmarks approaches, discusses robustness and practical deployment.

* **Contribution**:

  * Definitive **survey of inertial parameter identification methods**.
